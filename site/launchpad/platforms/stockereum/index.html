钱怎么收、怎么分 DefiLlama 口径原文（英文）：最能看出产品机制

手续费
The fee the Stockereum hook charges on every swap, in the quote currency, from the hook's Trade event: 1% on v2 launches, 1%, 2% or 3% (chosen by the creator) on v3 launches, plus the anti-snipe rate in the first moments after a launch.
协议收入
The platform's share of the swap fee: half of the normal 1% on v2, 0.5% of the trade on 1% v3 launches and 1% of the trade on 2% and 3% v3 launches, plus any anti-snipe excess.
留给协议 / 团队
The platform's share of the swap fee.
给创作者等供给方
The rest of the swap fee, paid to the launch's creator or, on launches with holder rewards enabled, to the launched token's holders.
