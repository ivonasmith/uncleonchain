钱怎么收、怎么分 DefiLlama 口径原文（英文）：最能看出产品机制

手续费
The 2% fee on every buy and sell of a coin launched on Sapling, the flat fee paid to plant a coin, and Sapling's fee on buys, plantings and sales made from a Zcash account. On Robinhood Chain: the 2% creator tax plus the creator's 70% of Pons's 1% fee on every buy and sell of a coin planted through Sapling, both paid to Sapling's splitter, plus the planting fee and the Zcash account fee, paid in USDG to the treasury. On Raydium LaunchLab (from 2026-10-28): any platform fee claimed from the curve and Sapling's fees from the coin's graduated pool; excludes the coin's transfer tax (Sapling's 2.00% plus the deployer's tax of none to 3%), which is taken on every transfer of the coin, wallet to wallet included.
协议收入
The part of the 2% coin trading fee Sapling keeps (10% treasury, 25% $SAPLING buyback and burn, 25% $SAPLING/ZEC liquidity), plus the planting fee and the Zcash account fee. For LaunchLab coins, all of the curve platform fee and the graduated pool's fees (a quarter each to the treasury, the flywheel, $SAPLING buyback and burn, and the protocol-owned $SAPLING/ZEC pool). On Robinhood Chain the shares apply to what reaches Sapling's splitter: the 2% creator tax plus the creator's 70% of Pons's 1% fee.
给持币人（回购 / 分红）
50% of the 2% coin trading fee: 25% buys and burns $SAPLING, and 25% goes to the $SAPLING/ZEC pool on PumpSwap, where about half of it buys $SAPLING, both sides are deposited and the LP tokens are burned, so no one, Sapling included, can withdraw it. On Robinhood Chain the shares apply to what reaches Sapling's splitter: the 2% creator tax plus the creator's 70% of Pons's 1% fee. For LaunchLab coins, half of the curve platform fee and the graduated pool's fees: $SAPLING buyback and burn, and the protocol-owned $SAPLING/ZEC pool, whose position is locked for good.
留给协议 / 团队
The treasury's share: 10% of the 2% coin trading fee, plus the planting fee and the Zcash account fee. For LaunchLab coins, the treasury's and the flywheel's quarters of the curve platform fee and the graduated pool's fees. On Robinhood Chain the shares apply to what reaches Sapling's splitter: the 2% creator tax plus the creator's 70% of Pons's 1% fee.
给创作者等供给方
40% of the 2% coin trading fee, paid to each coin's deployer, a wallet they choose, or the holders of that coin as holder rewards (on Robinhood Chain, 40% of what reaches Sapling's splitter). LaunchLab coins pay their deployers from the coin's transfer tax, which is not counted.
