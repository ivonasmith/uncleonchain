The only action-changing rule is '30-day average funding below zero' (crowded shorts, validated · direction): no new shorts, and when L2 is not in a bear market, scaling in within the L1 budget is allowed. Only 30-day average funding below zero passed: 31 independent events, up 74% of the time over the next 30 days (58% for the same era and cycle state), with shallower drawdowns and the same direction in all three eras.

Added four main gauges (STH cost +1σ pressure zone, STH cost reference support, supply concentration, 30-day volatility), a URPD supply map, and descriptive short-term-holder and whale/retail lines; exchange net flow and balance stay but are now descriptive only (no score, no tailwind/headwind colour). Only three rules passed: STH cost +1σ as a pressure zone (not broken within 30 days 63% vs 42% for placebos; 72% vs 44% since 2018), STH cost as reference support (near random lately), and low volatility + concentrated supply as a large-move warning (magnitude only, not direction).

Following the framework's own division of labour — the cycle sets direction, macro sets pace, flows and sentiment confirm and caution — it now combines all four layers.
