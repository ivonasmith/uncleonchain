2026-11-06
journal marker · changelog 2026.11.06		Launchpad matrix · delayed platformsEach platform's daily series now sets its last day; platforms not yet published keep their last value (so totals don't drop falsely) but are tagged 'carried MM-DD' in the ranking, home top 6, headline and day-over-day breakdown, with the number carried and their share noted; the next daily run swaps in the real value once DefiLlama publishes it. The rule is in Methodology › delayed platforms.DefiLlama's overview gives platforms that have not yet published the previous day their last day's value as 'today' (verified 2026-11-06: StonkFun had no Nov 5 data and the overview's 86,429 was its Nov 4 value). The matrix used it as the current day in the ranking, totals, day-over-day breakdown and share changes without any tag, and for non-top-60 platforms those cells were never corrected.

2026-11-02
journal marker · changelog 2026.11.02		Launchpad activity temperature · stagesAdded Slow cooling / Slow warming: when none of Cooling / Summer / Warming / Winter applies, a 14-day change ≤ −10% with the week still falling is Slow cooling, ≥ +10% with the week still rising is Slow warming; only the rest is Steady. 'Usually lasts' now skips stretches of ≤ 2 days and gives no range below 5 stretches. Daily stages were recomputed under the new rule (65 of 1,871 days changed label); stages.json is now v2.The old rule only checked whether the weekly change crossed ±10%; anything else was 'Steady (flat)'. From 2026-10-22 activity slid for two weeks ($3.73M → $3.39M 7-day average, temperature 41.6 → 35.3) without any single week falling 10%, yet the page kept saying 'flat'. Also, one-day threshold flickers counted as stretches and pulled 'usually lasts' too short.

journal marker · changelog 2026.10.29		L2 / L3 price basisL2 and L3 now use only closed UTC days: the data date moves from the run day to the previous day, and price = that day's 00:00 UTC close; the ruler and price bar now read 'close + close time'. L3's out-of-sample log is never rewritten: rows up to 2026-10-29 used the run-time price, later rows use the close.When Coin Metrics had not yet published the day, L2 and L3 used bitview's intraday price at run time, so the ruler's 'price' was a ~02:50 UTC intraday price, out of step with the rest of the site. Everything now uses the 00:00 UTC close.
2026-10-22
journal marker · changelog 2026.10.22		L4 Sentiment & DerivativesRebuilt per the L4 study and web spec: the L4 verdict now reads l4/l4_daily.py's level (signal / hot / warm / normal / cool). The only action-changing rule is '30-day average funding below zero' (crowded shorts, validated · direction): no new shorts, and when L2 is not in a bear market, scaling in within the L1 budget is allowed. The other eight readings (funding 30/7-day, quarterly basis, CME premium, aggregated OI 7-day change, OI ÷ market cap, Fear & Greed, DVOL) are temperature descriptions banded by their 730-day percentile; six watch items are hypotheses and change nothing. Retired the old thermometer (Hyperliquid funding, Hyperliquid OI 7-day change and a Fear & Greed score); the altseason index and BTC dominance became watch items outside the verdict. Logs up to 2026-10-22 stay unchanged.The old thermometer (OI 7-day change > funding > Fear & Greed) was never tested. The study tested the common claims on data since 2018 — high funding means a pullback, fear is a buy and greed a sell, OI build-ups cause cascades, low implied vol precedes big moves — and none held. Only 30-day average funding below zero passed: 31 independent events, up 74% of the time over the next 30 days (58% for the same era and cycle state), with shallower drawdowns and the same direction in all three eras.
2026-10-11
journal marker · changelog 2026.10.11		L3 Supply StructureRebuilt per the L3 supply study and the L3 web spec: the L3 verdict moved from '7-day exchange net flow' to 'price position relative to the STH cost +1σ pressure zone + a large-move warning'. Added four main gauges (STH cost +1σ pressure zone, STH cost reference support, supply concentration, 30-day volatility), a URPD supply map, and descriptive short-term-holder and whale/retail lines; exchange net flow and balance stay but are now descriptive only (no score, no tailwind/headwind colour). Removed the pending URPD, STH-MVRV, SOPR and miner-reserve cards (the first three are now live; miner reserves are not shown per the study). Data comes from l3/l3_daily.py daily at 02:47 UTC. Logs up to 2026-10-11 stay unchanged.Common supply and cost-line rules were tested on 16 years of data against same-shaped placebo lines at the wrong level: most lines did no better than placebos, and exchange flows and whale/retail holdings have no lead on the next 4 weeks. Only three rules passed: STH cost +1σ as a pressure zone (not broken within 30 days 63% vs 42% for placebos; 72% vs 44% since 2018), STH cost as reference support (near random lately), and low volatility + concentrated supply as a large-move warning (magnitude only, not direction).
2026-10-11
journal marker · changelog 2026.10.11		Overall readThe L3 paragraph of the overall read now describes price relative to the +1σ pressure zone and the large-move warning, instead of whether exchange flows agree with the cycle; the one-liner's 'flow divergence' note became 'price in the supply pressure zone' / 'large-move warning on'. Phrases live in config/composite.json.The study found exchange flows only move with price and have no lead, so judging whether they 'agree with the cycle' has no basis; what L3 can offer is the pressure zone and the large-move warning.
2026-10-08
journal marker · changelog 2026.10.08		L2 Cycle PositionL2 is now a rule-based state machine (L2 cycle study §7): Coin Metrics + bitview.space supply price, realized cap, age-band cost bases, holder and P/L data; each day it assigns one of six states (bull / bull top-risk / bull pullback / bear / bear bottom zone / late bear → early bull) with 14 signals and 13 key price levels. Removed the old single-gauge L2 cards (MVRV, MVRV Z, realized price, NUPL, Mayer, Puell, Pi Cycle) and the Pi Cycle alert; the L2 verdict is now the state, not the MVRV Z zone. Logs up to 2026-10-07 stay unchanged.The fixed thresholds of the single-gauge valuation cards all failed this cycle: MVRV bottomed at 1.10 and never broke 1; Pi Cycle never crossed; valuation peaked 9–10 months before price. L2 is now a state machine that uses only the three cross-cycle-tested signal groups (bottom zone, bottom confirmation, top-risk window). In the historical replay the phases separate cleanly: 'late bear → early bull' median +152% over the next year, 'bear' −36%.
2026-10-08
journal marker · changelog 2026.10.08		Overall readThe overall read moved from an L1 × L4 combination to a four-layer synthesis: one line = direction (L2 state) + pace (L1 regime and risk-budget action) + a flow divergence note (L3) + short-term temperature (L4), followed by four fixed paragraphs. Phrases, actions and headwind triggers live in config/composite.json. Logs from this day on use the new rule; older logs stay unchanged.The overall read used to combine only macro (L1) and sentiment (L4); cycle position and coin flows never entered it. Following the framework's own division of labour — the cycle sets direction, macro sets pace, flows and sentiment confirm and caution — it now combines all four layers.

2026-10-04
journal marker · changelog 2026.10.04		L1-B crypto funding channels'L1.5' renamed to 'L1-B crypto funding channels (confirm, don't predict)'. The stablecoin main line is now de-duplicated: total − payment − yield/synthetic (previously payment only); an incl.-Tron lens sits beside trading bullets; Tron and payment coins are watched separately; 52-week changes added. New: spot ETF 13-week flow and the rotation matrix (ETF 13w × trading bullets 13w); ETF labels unified as 'trend confirmation (lagging)'.Total stablecoin supply mixes in three kinds of money that is not there to trade: payment and institutional coins (−0.43 with BTC over 13 weeks), yield/synthetic coins minted against USDT/USDC (double-counted), and Tron stablecoins that barely track BTC. What is left after removing them looks like trading money. ETFs have become the main pipe for marginal buying (0.67 same-week correlation in the ETF era), so they sit with stablecoins. This group confirms but does not predict, so it is part of L1 rather than a layer of its own — hence 'L1-B'.
2026-10-02
journal marker · changelog 2026.10.02		L1 Macro LiquidityRebuilt per the L1 dimension spec: added Nasdaq 13w, VIX, optimism z, BAA/NFCI/HY 13w, real-yield states and scenario grid, net-liquidity gate, ex-payment / trading-bullet / yield stablecoin lines, ETF 4w, CME basis, BTC–Nasdaq 52w correlation and tokenized-stock monitor; removed USDT+USDC total, RRP and standalone Fed balance sheet; 2Y/10Y demoted to watch-only; real yield demoted to a state variable; Deribit basis now interpolated to a constant 90 days. L1 verdict changed from a score (expanding/neutral/contracting) to tailwind / neutral / cautious / headwind. Logs up to 2026-10-02 were written under the old rules and stay unchanged.Testing 16 years of data rule by rule showed that 'how much money was printed' barely leads BTC: only 1 of 14 lead relations survived out-of-sample, and M2's return correlation with BTC is just 0.08. What drives the weeks-to-months pace is market optimism and credit conditions. So L1 now puts optimism first, sets the regime with real yields and credit, and keeps liquidity only as a discount.
