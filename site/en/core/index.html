Leader picks

Each day, the top-100 Binance names are sorted into five classes by what really drives price, and the liquidity leaders of each are kept; five verifiable facts give a condition score and the top 10 are the default watch. The score measures how complete the conditions are, not a forecast; data and process only, not trade advice.
