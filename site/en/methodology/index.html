How layer verdicts are made

L1 Macro liquidity (since 2026-10-02, based on the BTC full-cycle macro attribution study): L1 sets volatility, pacing and risk budget over weeks to months, not direction.

  • Tailwind: optimism z > 0 and the BAA spread narrowing over 13 weeks, with no real-yield surge.
  • Neutral-cautious: a real-yield surge (13w ≥ +0.40pp) on its own — one notch less risk budget, no adds.
  • Headwind: a surge plus (BAA/NFCI tightening or optimism z < 0), or the liquidity gate (net liquidity 13w ≤ −2.72% with a stronger dollar) — nominal risk budget ×0.7, not compounded.
  • Neutral: everything else. An 'alert' tag is added when the high-yield spread widens ≥ 0.30pp over 13 weeks (early warning; this site's threshold, not back-tested).

L1-B crypto funding channels (confirm, don't predict): stablecoin main line (de-dup = total − payment − yield/synthetic), trading bullets (ex-Tron, with an incl.-Tron lens), spot ETFs (trend confirmation, lagging), futures basis (CME and Deribit side by side) and the rotation matrix (ETF 13w × trading bullets 13w). Agreement with price confirms, divergence warns; not part of the regime verdict.

The real yield is no longer L1's first gauge (optimism is); it is a state variable that picks the row of the scenario matrix and sets the regime together with credit. Whether each rule may change trading parameters is copied rule by rule from §10.2 of the macro study — see the L1 rule list below. 13-week changes are aligned to Friday closes. Thresholds use post-2018 data only.

L2 Cycle: A rule-based state machine (l2/l2_signals.py, L2 cycle study §7) stepped day by day: new high → bull; halving +480–600 days or MVRV ≥ 2 with price/200W MA ≥ 2 → bull top-risk; drawdown ≥ 20% → bull pullback (back to bull inside 10%); ≥ 35% with the high ≥ 90 days old and the halving ≥ 450 days old → bear; ≥ 2 bottom-zone signals in a bear → bottom zone; +40% from a ≥ 90-day-old low, or 7 days above four short-term cost lines with an age-band confirmation this cycle → late bear → early bull; a close below the cycle low reverts to bear. Data: Coin Metrics community API + bitview.space. Layer verdict = the state of the day.

L3 Coin flows: Based on 7-day exchange net flow: < −2,000 BTC = outflow, > +2,000 = inflow (watch selling), otherwise balanced. Exchange balance change is shown as trend only. L3 is being rebuilt.

L4 Sentiment: Fear & greed (−2..+2), funding (−1..+2) and 7-day OI change (−1..+1): ≥ 3 hot, 1–2 warm, ≤ −2 cool, otherwise neutral. L4 research is unfinished; it only describes temperature.

How the overall read is made

The overall read combines four layers along the study's division of labour: L2 sets the direction; L1 sets pace and risk budget, not direction; L3 checks whether flows agree with the cycle; L4 only describes short-term temperature and never changes the action (its research is not finished).

One line = '{direction}; {but} {pace} — {action}. {flow divergence} {short term}': 'but' is added when the direction leans up while L1 is cautious/headwind, or leans down / top-risk while L1 is a tailwind; actions come only from L1's risk-budget rules and the L2 hand-over principle ('no rush to turn bearish on direction; no chasing on pace, scale in per L1's budget'); a divergence note is added when L3 disagrees; L4 adds its temperature when not neutral. Lines over 60 characters drop the short-term note first, then the divergence note (both stay in the paragraphs).

What the tags mean

Level and evidence grade are two separate dimensions: level is the gauge's role in the layer verdict, evidence grade is how well the research has tested it.

Level (solid tag)
  • coreFeeds the layer verdict.
  • auxShown for context; not part of the verdict.
  • watchTested but not good enough; watch only.
  • state variableNot scored on its own; picks the row of the scenario grid.
Evidence grade (outlined tag)
  • VerifiedMeasured on our own data and passed noise / robustness checks.
  • Verified · directionDirection is stable, but samples are few or magnitudes unstable.
  • DescriptiveDescribes where things stand; sets no parameters.
  • MonitorSize or definition is still changing; recorded for now.
  • Watch onlyTested but not usable.
  • HypothesisReasoned mechanism, or holds only in some cycles.
  • FalsifiedTested and failed; shown only to explain why it is not used.
  • RecordingSelf-recorded for fewer than 90 days; no percentiles yet.
Colour (by what it means for BTC)
  • TailwindHistorically followed by gains, or supportive.
  • NeutralFlat, calm or mid-range.
  • HeadwindHistorically followed by losses, or a drag.
  • WatchWorth watching, descriptive or still recording.

Log rules

Log date = UTC day of the run (the daily run is at 10:20 UTC); each reading carries its own data date (FRED on US business days, NFCI weekly, exchange balance ~2 weeks late; stale readings get ⚠). Frozen once written; a same-day re-run only overwrites when fewer core inputs are missing, marked 'Re-run'. Weekly reviews follow ISO weeks, monthly ones calendar months; switches on a rule-change day are labelled as such. Wherever log content appears elsewhere it is marked with its freeze time; the latest values win.

Launchpad conventions

MeasureLaunchpads are always compared by fees, never volume (volume coverage is incomplete and understates share).
PeriodOnly 'yesterday' as a full UTC day is reported; today's data is never complete.
MatrixAll protocols in DefiLlama's Launchpad category, split by chain with DefiLlama's per-chain data; the sector trend uses daily top-60 fees since 2024-01.
ShareShare bars = % of the filtered total (chain × period × tier); deep-tracked share uses the top-60 daily total.
Late dataA source that has not published yesterday is marked late — never back-filled with old values or zero.
Flap / StonkFunFlap is a tax-token model and StonkFun fees count only the platform's cut; compare them by revenue.
PONS burnsSent to 0x…dEaD rather than burn(); market cap is always burn-adjusted.
NeverNo 'it's dead' calls from comparing with all-time peaks; data only, no trade advice.
