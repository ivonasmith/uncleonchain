How it differs
Per official docs, o1 Launchpad offers launches on Base, Robinhood, BSC, Monad, X Layer, Arc, Elysium and others. Tokens have a fixed 1B supply and open a permanently locked Uniswap v4 pool immediately, without the creator depositing the paired asset. Supports Standard (fixed fee split), Tax (custom taxes) and $O (wstO-paired with automatic burns) modes, and can pair with stock tokens. Part of the o1.exchange ecosystem.
Fee split
Per official docs, Standard base swap fee is 1%, split 50% creator, 30% platform, 20% referrer. Tax mode lets the creator set buy/sell taxes and allocate the remainder. $O launches default to burning 1% of the quote-side wstO on buy and sell. Creation fees vary by chain (0.001 ETH on Base/Robinhood Standard/Tax, none for $O, etc.). Anti-snipe decays from 99% to 1% over about 20 seconds on most chains. Liquidity is permanently locked and non-removable.
Token
Has platform token $O. Per DefiLlama and X, it has market cap and volume. $O launches are linked to wstO and support burn mechanics.
Team & backing
Per web reports and X, founders are Jerry Pan (@stambouli_o1) and Ethan Chang. Backed by Coinbase Ventures, Alliance, a16z and others, with funding in the millions of dollars.

How fees are charged and split DefiLlama methodology (original): the clearest view of the mechanics

Fees
Quote-denominated swap fees from Hook Trade events plus token-launch payment events across configured suites. Legacy launch-token-denominated swap fees are excluded because reliable historical USD valuation is unavailable. Crypto uses token balances; Stocks use the event-time factory tick reference price under the documented $4,000 opening-cap convention, with historical DefiLlama prices only when that reference is unavailable.
Revenue
Actual platform and protocol-owned fixed-component swap credits, plus token-launch fees. Includes anti-snipe surcharges, referral fallback and rounding retained by the protocol. Claims do not count again.
Kept by protocol / team
Swap and launch fees retained by the protocol treasury.
To creators / supply side
Actual swap credits allocated to creators and referrers, including amounts still unclaimed.
