档案

New token = minted in a round supply (a multiple of 1,000) in one transaction; factory = the contract the creating transaction called; tokens deployed directly by individuals are counted apart. Creators = distinct creating addresses among sampled transactions (a lower bound). New = first seen in the last 14 days; ramping = ≥ 50 new tokens in 7 days and at least 2× the prior 7 days. Generic contracts (account-abstraction entry points, multicall) are excluded; names come from verified contracts or manual labels, otherwise “unnamed contract”. Describes what is being launched; not a recommendation.
