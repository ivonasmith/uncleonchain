How is this calculated?

Temperature: take the past two years of daily activity (7-day average launchpad fees), drop the lowest and highest 5% of days, stretch the rest to 0–100 and see where today lands. 0–20 low, 20–40 cool, 40–60 moderate, 60–80 warm, 80–100 high.

Stage: weekly change ≤ −10% is Cooling; else temperature ≥ 70 Summer; else change ≥ +10% Warming; else temperature ≤ 20 Winter; otherwise Steady. Each day uses only data up to that day.

Wave: the 7-day average stays above 1.5× the prior four-week norm (median) for 3 days to start; it ends after 7 straight days back within 1.2× the norm or below 60% of the wave’s peak. “Hottest stretch” = days around the peak within 80% of it.
