How are the dial and stages calculated?

Dial uses the historical Q05–Q95 amplitude position, displayed within 0–100 with unclipped values separately. Temperature bands are low, cool, moderate, warm and high at 20-point intervals. Stage priority: weekly change ≤−10% cooling; otherwise position ≥70 summer; otherwise change ≥10% warming; otherwise position ≤20 winter; otherwise consolidation. Missing values are unclassified.

Stage mapping is a new reviewable display rule, not validated for profit or peak exits. Historical high-region duration is not called summer duration.

Method and audit boundaries

Early launchpad data includes fundraising platforms; DEX covers many asset classes. They are not stitched into a nine-year MEME index. DOGE address activity is retained only in the original research attachment and excluded from this panel and temperature readings. New venues, chains, versions and backfills can alter aggregates—even with a fixed venue list. Fee share is not volume share.

Historical rules: 7d smoothing, 28d baseline, 1.5× for 3 days to confirm entry; at or below 1.2× baseline or 60% of the running peak for 7 days to confirm exit. High-region analysis uses an 80% peak rule. Starts, peaks and high regions include hindsight; smoothing changes durations. These are not exit deadlines.

The scale excludes the outer 5% at each end, while retaining all raw extremes. Fewer than 365 valid historical days means no long-term scale. Season labels are provisional presentation rules, not validated trading findings.
